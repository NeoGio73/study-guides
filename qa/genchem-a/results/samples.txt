=== molar:molar
Q: What is the molar mass of sulfur dioxide (SO2)? Give your answer to two decimal places.
ANS: 64.06 g/mol
GUIDED: Step 1: count the atoms of each element. Step 2: multiply by the atomic mass. ¶ S atoms×32.06 g/mol= g/mol ¶ O atoms×15.999 g/mol= g/mol ¶ Step 3: add the subtotals. ¶
HINTS: Hint 1 ¶ The molar mass of a compound is the sum of the atomic masses of all the atoms in its formula. A subscript counts the atoms of the element just before it. ¶ || Hint 2: the atoms in one molecule ¶ 1 S, 2 O ¶ S? atoms×32.06 g/mol=? g/mol ¶ O? atoms×15.999 g/mol=? g/mol ¶
SOLUTION: Worked solutionCount the atoms in one molecule of SO2: 1 S, 2 O. ¶ Multiply and add.S1 atom×32.06 g/mol=32.060 g/mol ¶ O2 atoms×15.999 g/mol=31.998 g/mol ¶ ¶ Molar mass = 32.060 + 31.998 = 64.06 g/mol ¶ ¶ One mole of SO2 has a mass of 64.06 g. ¶ ¶
